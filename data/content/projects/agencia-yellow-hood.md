## Description

YellowHood is a brazilian digital agency that was born from a collaborative effort between a group of friends who shared the same vision: to build meaningful digital experiences and help brands establish a strong online presence.

What started as individual freelance projects quickly evolved into something bigger. As our work grew and the demands became more complex, YellowHood was created to professionalize our freelance services, unify our skills, and deliver higher-quality results with consistency and strategy.

YellowHood is not just an agency — it’s the evolution of our freelance journey into a structured, collaborative, and professional digital studio.
